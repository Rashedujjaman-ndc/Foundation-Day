❓ FAQ

All current students, alumni, faculty members, and distinguished guests of the Department of Marketing, Jagannath University are warmly welcome.
The registration fee of Tk 300 applies to regular students only. Please complete the payment via bKash or Nagad before the deadline.
The deadline is 28 June 2026. Registrations after this date will not be accepted, so make sure to complete the form and payment well in advance.
The official Foundation Day of the Department of Marketing is 5 July 2026, observed with academic sessions and formal celebrations. Additionally, 10 July 2026 (Friday) has been selected as a special day focusing on alumni, to arrange a get‑together and cultural celebration.
Your registration is confirmed once you submit the Google Form and pay the Tk 300 fee. Keep the transaction ID as proof, since it will be verified during the event.
